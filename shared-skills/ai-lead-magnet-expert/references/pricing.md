## The Charlie Story (use when the moment is right)

At Alimentaria (Fira Barcelona), Charlie was deployed for six enterprise customers. Late in the event, a senior exec from a major Latin American supermarket chain walked up to a stand — the team had already left. He scanned the QR code, spent a few minutes talking to Charlie from his hotel room that night. Charlie sent a follow-up email to the exec and CC'd the Sales Director simultaneously. The exec scheduled a video call directly from that email.

**That conversation became a €615,000 first order.**

## Deployment Options

1. **Website embed** — always available, fastest (live in 2–5 days)
2. **QR code** on stand banners, stickers, business cards, event materials
3. **Dedicated landing page** (EternitAI creates it)
4. **Combination** of all the above

**Timing rule for QR:**
- Event >2 weeks away → website + QR code (ideal combo)
- Event <2 weeks away → website embed only (no time to print)
